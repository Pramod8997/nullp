4. REQUIRED MAINS-SAFETY ITEMS NOT INCLUDED IN THE PRICE TOTAL
---------------------------------------------------------------

The following are required before any 230 V connection, but no exact
price-verified listing has been approved here:

- Proper flame-retardant electrical enclosure.
- Mains-rated inlet and switched output socket.
- 5 A ceramic fuse and correctly rated fuse holder.
- 1.0 mm² mains conductors and strain relief.
- Protective-earth conductor, PE terminal and continuity hardware.
- Correctly rated terminal blocks/connectors.
- Upstream 30 mA RCBO/RCCB.
- BIS-marked isolated 5 V 2 A supply and a USB-A-female-to-screw-terminal
  breakout for the final low-voltage rail (not price-verified here).

These items are not optional. Do not energize the PZEM, relay or socket from
230 V until the mains wiring, earthing, insulation, enclosure and protection
have been physically checked by a competent person. The software simulator
and automated tests do not validate those physical safety properties.
